Understanding Climate Change

Climate change refers to long term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns, largely driven by human activity since the industrial era. Burning fossil fuels such as coal, oil, and gas releases greenhouse gases that trap heat in the atmosphere, gradually raising the planet's average temperature.

The effects of climate change include rising sea levels, more frequent extreme weather events, shifting rainfall patterns, and disruption to ecosystems. Coastal cities face increased flooding risk, while some regions experience longer droughts that threaten agriculture and water supplies.

Governments and organizations around the world are working on strategies to reduce emissions, including transitioning to renewable energy sources like solar and wind power, improving energy efficiency, and protecting forests that absorb carbon dioxide. Individual actions, such as reducing waste and energy consumption, also contribute to broader climate goals.
