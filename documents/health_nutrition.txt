Basics of Health and Nutrition

Good nutrition provides the body with the energy and nutrients needed to function properly. A balanced diet typically includes carbohydrates for energy, proteins for muscle repair and growth, healthy fats for hormone production, and a variety of vitamins and minerals that support immune function and overall wellbeing.

Regular physical activity works alongside good nutrition to maintain a healthy body weight, strengthen the cardiovascular system, and improve mental health. Even moderate exercise, such as brisk walking for thirty minutes a day, has been shown to reduce the risk of chronic diseases like heart disease and diabetes.

Sleep quality is another often overlooked factor in overall health. Poor sleep can affect concentration, mood, and metabolism, while consistent, quality sleep supports recovery and cognitive performance. Together, nutrition, exercise, and sleep form the foundation of a healthy lifestyle.
