An Overview of Cricket

Cricket is a bat and ball sport played between two teams of eleven players. It originated in England and is now hugely popular in countries such as India, Australia, Pakistan, and England. A match involves one team batting to score runs while the other team bowls and fields, trying to dismiss the batting side.

There are several formats of cricket, including Test matches that last up to five days, One Day Internationals limited to fifty overs per side, and the fast paced Twenty20 format that has grown enormously in popularity due to leagues like the Indian Premier League. Each format demands different strategies from players and captains.

Cricket combines individual skill, such as batting technique and bowling accuracy, with team strategy around field placements and match tactics. Major tournaments, including the Cricket World Cup, draw massive global audiences and have produced many legendary players over the decades.
